## Customize

Use **Presets** to search by anime, theme or effect and choose a rendered preview.
A preset is one Undo step. Controller position is preserved. With **Include preset
wordmark** enabled, a small grip title and its typeface/placement are applied.
Disable it to keep your existing logo/text, font, size, angle and position.

Under **Effects → Signature artwork**, mix ABXY artwork, Home emblems and shell
details independently. Shell detail strength affects the engraving and its motion.
Button artwork intentionally replaces face colors with its themed material;
individual pressed colors light the rim. Choose Original button faces to restore
normal face coloring. A/B/X/Y labels can be hidden. Remove theme artwork preserves
colors, generic effects, artwork and layout.

**Motion** has Full, Reduced and Still modes. Reduced slows animation and softens
shell effects. Still freezes animation while live presses still change colors.
New designs follow the operating system's reduced-motion preference. Presets keep
your motion preference and label setting. Themes enable energy rings; turn them
off anytime in Effects. Neutral and older projects keep rings off by default.

**Energy rings on button press** triggers once on each rising input edge, expires
within 700 ms, and rearms after release. It does not keep firing while held.
Rings and shell graphics are masked away from physical controls and transparent
background. The same painter is embedded in HTML and local OBS exports. PNG
captures one still. Republish and refresh OBS after editing a published overlay.

These are fan-inspired geometric treatments, not licensed character illustrations
or official franchise/GameSir skins. The supplied G7 shell's existing faint baked
reference artwork remains an inherited limitation. Physical controller and OBS
verification on Windows is still needed.
