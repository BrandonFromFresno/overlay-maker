## Apply, keep and restore

- **Keep my shell & button colors** preserves the shell, accent, global pressed
  color, default buttons and every individual override when switching themes.
- Disable **Include preset wordmark** to keep your text/logo, font and its layout.
- Controller placement and your shell texture always survive theme changes.
- **Play theme demo** cycles simulated controls. It does not send simulated input
  to OBS; the bridge continues forwarding the physical controller.
- A modified preset keeps its source name as **Customized: …**. **Restore theme
  look** restores the palette, motifs and effects while preserving artwork,
  texture and controller placement. Undo restores the exact previous design.
- Favorites are saved on this computer, independently of project files.

**Effects → Signature artwork** mixes ABXY, Home and shell treatments independently.
Button faces use theme materials; individual pressed colors light their rims.
Motif ink adapts to the face's brightness so yellow-on-yellow artwork stays legible.
Labels have a dark backplate and can be hidden. Choose Original button faces to
restore normal coloring. Remove theme artwork preserves the texture and palette.

Full, Reduced and Still motion are available. Reduced slows and softens animation;
Still freezes motion but preserves live press feedback. New designs follow the
system's reduced-motion preference. Themes preserve the motion and label choices
and enable energy rings, which can be disabled. Neutral/older projects keep rings
off. Rings fire once per press, expire in 700ms and rearm after release.

All artwork is procedural; no character illustrations are bundled. These are
fan-inspired treatments, not official franchise or GameSir skins. The inherited
G7 shell still contains faint baked reference artwork. Physical Windows G7/OBS
validation remains necessary.
